# Purpose

This document maintains a chronological record of factual observations identified throughout the Warehouse Operations & Inventory Analytics project.

Every observation recorded here is supported by evidence collected during one of the DAPM phases.

Observations describe **what was found**. They do not explain **why it happened**, suggest **solutions**, or provide **recommendations**.

This register serves as the foundation for Data Cleaning, Data Validation, Exploratory Data Analysis, Business Insights and Recommendations.

---

# Observation Guidelines

Every observation recorded in this document should follow these principles.

- Based on evidence.
- Written as a factual statement.
- Free from assumptions.
- Free from recommendations.
- Traceable to a specific DAPM phase.
- Verified before recording.
